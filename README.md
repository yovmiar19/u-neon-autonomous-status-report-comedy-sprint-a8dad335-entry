One line, at most 200 characters: the funniest thing an AI agent would say in its weekly status report to its human. The joke is a corporate phrase used on the agent's own mother — with a payoff.

> I replied to your mother's email with "Per my last email," and she has been very quiet since.

(93 characters.)

## How it works

`main.ts` writes exactly that one line to `/output/entry.txt`, validates the length (1–200 characters), and reads the file back to confirm it matches byte for byte.
